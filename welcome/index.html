New: The Field

No road. You build the path.

On The Field, monsters take the shortest way across the grass. Every hero and every wall you place bends that way, and the dotted lines show where the next wave will walk.

  • 🧱Walls make monsters walk longer under your heroes' reach. You just can't seal the School.
  • 🪽Flyers ignore your maze. Only heroes with air reach stop them.
  • 🛋The Couch Giant smashes through a wall when the detour is too long.
  • 🌀A second portal opens halfway through. Plan for it.
